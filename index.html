Questions

Does it replace the engineer?

No. You author the section and keep every parameter, target and code in front of you. AMAYA builds it and makes sure Civil 3D can load it.

Is it a chatbot inside Civil 3D?

No. It is a desktop app that writes the .pkt. The output is the file, not the conversation.

What if AMAYA cannot build my section?

The beta is supervised. If a section does not come out right, send it to me and I fix what breaks. Every failing piece becomes a check inside AMAYA.

Do I need to know coding?

No. You only need your engineering background. AMAYA takes care of everything else.
